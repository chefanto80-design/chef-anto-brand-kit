# Chef Anto Brand Kit

The single source of truth for how Chef Anto looks and sounds. Every other chef-anto-* skill (LinkedIn, newsletter, pop-up ops, website) follows this kit. If a request conflicts with the kit, point it out and ask.

## 3. Voice
- **Feels like:** a grandmother's Balkan kitchen meets modern tech. Warm, personal, **cozy**, confident, never over the top.
- **Do:** tell a small true story, use sensory words (warm bread, dill, paprika), speak to one person, end with a question or one clear CTA.
- **Don't:** hype ("revolutionary", "game-changer", "hurry", "deal"), corporate jargon, AI-sounding text (run the humanizer skill if available), fake reviews or invented press.
- **Credentials (real, OK to use):** International Culinary Center (New York & Geneva); ABC Kitchen; NoHo Hospitality Group (ABC V opening team); Kellari Hospitality Group; Art Basel Miami events.

## 4. Colors (confirm with Chef Anto before first use; then keep consistent)
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm cream | `#F6EFE4` |
| Primary | Deep olive | `#3F4A2E` |
| Accent (CTA only) | Paprika | `#B5482A` |
| Text | Ink | `#1F1B16` |
| Soft neutral | Linen | `#E7DCCB` |
Rules: max 3 core colors per design; paprika only for buttons and highlights; always check text contrast (WCAG AA).

## 5. Typography
- **Headings:** a refined serif (Fraunces or Cormorant Garamond).
- **Body:** clean sans (Inter or system font).
- Max 2 fonts. Headlines short; lots of white space.

## 6. Imagery
Hands cooking, wooden tables, herbs, bread, Balkan ceramics, colorful fridges, Miami natural light, Chef Anto smiling in the kitchen. Avoid cold stock photos, robots, neon, glossy AI gradients.
